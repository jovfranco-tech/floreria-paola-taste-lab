# Design Evaluation Framework

This document defines how to compare the baseline and redesigned Florería Paola AR experiences without reducing the review to personal taste.

## Review method

Evaluate both versions at the same viewport sizes and with the same tasks:

1. Identify what the business sells.
2. Find a relevant service or occasion.
3. Understand where the business operates.
4. Request a quote.
5. Locate contact alternatives.
6. Navigate back to the comparison index.

Recommended viewport checks:

- 390 × 844 mobile
- 768 × 1024 tablet
- 1440 × 900 desktop

## Scoring rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Value proposition | Unclear | Understandable after reading | Immediate and specific |
| Visual hierarchy | Competing elements | Mostly ordered | Clear priority and scanning path |
| Trust | Little evidence | Basic local-business credibility | Strong, consistent credibility cues |
| Conversion path | Hard to find | Available | Contextual, visible, and low friction |
| Navigation | Confusing | Functional | Predictable across desktop and mobile |
| Accessibility | Major barriers | Basic semantics | Clear labels, focus, alternatives, and contrast |
| Responsiveness | Layout breaks | Usable | Purposeful adaptation across sizes |
| Content density | Overwhelming or sparse | Acceptable | Balanced and task-oriented |
| Maintainability | Repeated and coupled | Partially structured | Reusable patterns and clear sections |

## Evidence to capture

For a useful design review, record:

- screenshots at each viewport;
- task completion notes;
- visible layout or overflow defects;
- keyboard-navigation observations;
- accessibility concerns;
- unclear or unsupported claims;
- conversion actions and their context;
- differences that materially affect confidence or comprehension.

## Claims boundary

A higher design score is not proof of higher sales or conversion. Business impact requires production analytics, representative traffic, agreed success metrics, and a controlled measurement period.

This lab provides design evidence only.
